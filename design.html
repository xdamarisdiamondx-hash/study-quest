§3 Colour

Three ramps, and nothing else

Colour is scarce on purpose, so when it appears it means something. The accent carries action and progress, gold carries reward, and warm neutrals carry everything else.

§9 Buttons

Pill-shaped, 46 px, one primary per screen

Fully rounded, Inter 500, with a two-layer focus ring that stays visible on every surface. Hover deepens the fill; nothing shifts position.

Variants

VariantUse for
primary iris-600The one action the screen wants you to take. Once.
secondary card + borderEverything else that matters on this screen.
ghost transparentLow-commitment actions, cancel, dismiss.
danger transparent + bad-500Delete, discard, wipe. Never filled — filled red invites misclicks.
reward gold-500Claiming an achievement. The only filled gold in the app.

§9 Inputs

46 px, pill-shaped, label always visible

Every field has a real <label> above it — placeholder text is a hint, never a label. Focus is iris-400 with a 22 % ring; errors are bad-500 with a message underneath, not colour alone.
