The one rule

One accent hue. Everything else is a tint of it or a warm neutral.

Colour is scarce on purpose, so when it appears it means something. Gradients are atmosphere — they sit behind hero areas and empty states, never on a button, a progress bar or anything you must read.

Accent

One violet. Buttons, active states, progress, focus. That is all of it.

Reward

Gold, one element per screen. XP, levels, streaks. Never a button.

Status

Three, desaturated. Only on error, overdue, completion.

Subjects are no longer colour-coded. A subject is a quiet neutral tile with its monogram; the accent appears only on the one that is active. Multi-series charts use tints of the single accent, not a rainbow.

§2 Brand

One mark, used everywhere

An open book beneath a gold quest star, on a violet gradient. The favicon and monochrome files are technical derivatives of this one mark, not alternative designs. The wordmark lockup is for the README only — the app uses the mark beside an already-typeset product name.

§12 Accessibility

Seven requirements, part of done

Not a later pass. And cutting the palette must not cut legibility — sand-500 on white is 4.6:1 and is the lightest permitted body-adjacent text.

#Requirement
1Keyboard reachable and operable, visible focus ring everywhere
2Touch targets at least 44 × 44 px
3Progress exposes role="progressbar" and aria-valuenow"
4Quiz runner announces questions and results via a live region
5Colour is never the only signal — always paired with a label or icon
6prefers-reduced-motion honoured
7AA contrast in both light and dark themes

Use the toggle in the header — every surface above is defined for both themes, and nothing is a lighter-than-legible grey in either.
